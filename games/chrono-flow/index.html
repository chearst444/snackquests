How to Play

You're an operator in a data-processing facility. Draw one continuous path from Start through every Data Node to the Target — before the timer runs out.

StartWhere your path begins.
TargetFinish here with every data node charged to clear the level.
Data NodeYour path must pass through every one of these.
Live ProcessorSafe to route your path through.
Inactive ProcessorPowered down — blocks the path. Appears from Tier 2 on.
CapacitorYou can cross it, but it starts draining the moment you arrive — move on before it overloads. Tier 3.
JunctionThe only tile your path may cross itself on. Rare, Tier 3 levels.

Controls: drag from your path's current end to extend it, or tap each next tile one at a time — both work, even mixed together. Drag back over your own trail to undo a step.
