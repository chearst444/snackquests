Why there are no ads in the middle of a level

We make games you can actually finish. That means no pop-up ads, no hidden trackers, and no surprise microtransactions. If a Snack Quest costs something, you will see the price up front — like a candy bar on a shelf, not a slot machine in a waiting room.

  • No pop-up ads. A puzzle should stay a puzzle. We will not interrupt a level to sell you something else.
  • No hidden trackers. This storefront does not load analytics pixels or ad networks. The games are meant to be handed to a kid without a second thought about who is watching.
  • No surprise microtransactions. Titles are priced like gas-station candy: $0.99–$2.99, or a Snack Pack bundle at $4.99. Paid downloads will come via itch.io.
